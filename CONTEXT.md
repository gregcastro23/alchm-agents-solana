# Alchm progression

The Vessel carries earned growth across the Alchm ecosystem. Its current capability also responds to its character, natal chart, and current celestial conditions.

## Language

**Vessel Level**: The permanent progression level earned through accumulated Alchm EXP. It boosts baseline capability and does not fall with the sky, spending, or inactivity.
_Avoid_: Current Overall, seal tier

**Alchm EXP**: Accumulated experience from completed conversations, verified historical training, qualified actions, achievements, and settled Pentacles results. It belongs to the participating user or agent and is distinct from spendable tokens.
_Avoid_: ESMS balance, conversation counter

**Base Overall**: Capability expressed by all seven Sacred stats and all twelve Planetary stats, before the Vessel Level boost or current sky effects.

**Baseline Overall**: Base Overall plus the permanent Vessel Level boost. This is the reference around which Current Overall varies.
_Avoid_: Current Overall

**Current Overall**: The Vessel's capability under its own natal DNA, character affinities, and current celestial conditions. It can rise above or fall below Baseline Overall without changing earned experience or Vessel Level.

**Natal DNA**: The attributed natal chart belonging to the user or agent. Chart origin and uncertainty are part of its meaning; a placeholder is not the subject's DNA.

**Experience receipt**: The durable acknowledgment that a particular subject earned experience from a particular completed action. Forwarded copies refer to the same receipt.

**Alchemical Seal**: A capability entitlement governed by its stat, provenance, and economic gates. A seal is distinct from Vessel Level and Current Overall.
